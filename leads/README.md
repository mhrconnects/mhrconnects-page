# Manchester website leads

`manchester-leads.csv` lists 29 local businesses in Manchester that appear to have **no website** (Facebook, Instagram, booking-app or directory listing only) or a **weak one**. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

- **Priority A** – good evidence of no website. Contact these first.
- **Priority B** – website status not confirmed, or they have a site that may be dated.

> These come from search-engine listings, not a visit to each page. Before contacting anyone, do the 30-second check below.

## 30-second check per lead
1. Google `"<business name>" Manchester`. Look for a website link on their Google Business profile.
2. If there's no site → **no-website lead**.
3. If there is a site, open it on your phone and look for these problems:
   - no padlock (no HTTPS)
   - text too small, or you have to zoom
   - old copyright year in the footer
   - no menu, prices or booking
   - takes more than about 3 seconds to load
   - broken images or links

   Two or more problems → **bad-website lead**.

## Opening message (WhatsApp / Facebook DM / phone)
> Hi <name>, I'm a web designer in Manchester. I noticed <business> has great reviews on Facebook but doesn't have its own website, so people searching Google for "<trade> in <area>" won't find you. I build simple, mobile-friendly sites for local businesses — happy to mock up a free homepage so you can see what it would look like. Interested?

For takeaways, add: *"…with your own online menu and ordering, so you don't pay 15–30% commission to the delivery apps."*

**Tip:** sending a free one-page mock-up with their name, logo and photos converts far better than a cold pitch.
