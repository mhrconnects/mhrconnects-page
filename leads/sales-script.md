# Sales script: selling websites to local businesses

Replace anything in `[brackets]`. Keep it short, friendly and local. You're a Manchester person helping a Manchester business.

---

## 1. First message (Facebook / Instagram DM or WhatsApp)

**Trades (roofers, plumbers, builders)**
> Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], a web designer here in Manchester. I came across [Business] on Facebook. [300+ five-star reviews / your job photos] are brilliant, but I couldn't find a website for you. That means when someone Googles "roofer in [area]", they're finding your competitors instead.
>
> I've put together a free sample homepage for you, no strings attached. Here's a screenshot 👇
> [attach screenshot]
>
> Would you like me to send you the full version to look at on your phone?

**Takeaways / restaurants**
> Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], a local web designer. Love the look of your [food item] on Facebook! I noticed you don't have your own website, so customers either can't find your menu or they order through Just Eat/Uber Eats and you lose up to 30% on every order.
>

> [attach screenshot]
>

>
> I made a free sample site for [Business] with your gallery and a booking button 👇

## 2. Follow-ups (if no reply)

**After 2 days**
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send the sample over, no pressure at all 👍

**After 5 more days (last one)**
> Hi [Name], last message from me! If a website isn't a priority right now, no worries at all. If it ever is, give me a shout. All the best with the business.

Stop there. Never send more than 3 messages.

---

## 3. Phone script (cold call or when they reply "call me")

**Opening (first 10 seconds matter most)**
> "Hi, is that [Name]? It's [Your name]. I'm a local web designer in Manchester. I'm not selling anything right now. I actually made a free sample website for [Business] and wanted to see if you'd like me to send it over. Have you got 30 seconds?"

**If yes**
> "Great. I saw you've got [great reviews / loads of happy customers] on Facebook, but there's no website, so when people search Google for [trade] in [area], you're not showing up. Your competitors are getting those calls.
>
> I've built a sample with your name, your services and a quote form so customers can contact you 24/7. What's the best number or email to send it to?"

**Ask questions (let THEM talk)**
- "Where do most of your new customers come from at the moment?"
- "Do you ever get people asking if you've got a website?"
- "If you got [5] more jobs a month, could you handle them?"

**Close**
> "So here's how it works: I set the whole thing up for you, with your photos, your reviews and your own web address like [business].co.uk. You don't have to do anything technical. It's £[price] to set up, and then £[monthly] a month for hosting and any changes you need. Shall I get it started for you?"

## 4. Handling objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"I get enough work from Facebook / word of mouth."** | "That's brilliant, it shows you do good work. But think about people who don't know anyone who's used you. They Google, and they can't find you. A website means you get those customers too, not just referrals." |
| **"It's too expensive."** | "I get that. If the site brings in just one extra [roof repair / 10 extra orders] a month, it's paid for itself. And I can spread the cost so you pay monthly." |
| **"I already have a website."** | "Oh, nice! Could I have a quick look? A lot of older sites don't work well on phones, and that's where 70%+ of people search now. I'll tell you honestly if it's fine." |
| **"I'll think about it."** | "Of course. What's the main thing you'd want to think over? [Answer it.] I'll keep the sample saved for you. Shall I check back with you on [day]?" |
| **"Just Eat / Fresha / Facebook is enough."** | "They're great for getting seen, but you're renting space on their platform and paying their fees. A website is yours: your customers, your bookings, no commission." |
| **"I'm too busy."** | "That's exactly why I do everything for you. I just need 10 minutes of your time to grab some photos and details, and I handle the rest." |
| **"Who are you? Is this a scam?"** | "Fair question! I'm a local designer. Here's my [website / portfolio / Facebook]. You don't pay anything until you've seen and approved the site." |

## 5. Pricing guide (Manchester small business)

| Package | Setup | Monthly | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | £299–£499 | £20–£30 | 1–3 page site, mobile-friendly, contact/quote form, Google Business profile set up |
| **Business** | £600–£900 | £30–£50 | 5+ pages, gallery, reviews, basic local SEO, small updates each month |
| **Takeaway** | £500–£800 | £30–£50 | Online menu + direct ordering link, opening hours, map |

Tip: for your first 2–3 clients, offer a discount in exchange for a Google review and permission to show their site as a portfolio piece. Then raise your prices.

---

## 6. After they say yes

1. Send a short written agreement: price, what's included, and the monthly fee.
2. Take a 50% deposit before you start.
3. Collect their logo, photos, phone number, services, prices and 3 reviews.
4. Deliver within 7 days, get their approval, then take the remaining 50%.
5. Ask for a review and a referral: "Do you know any other business owners who need a website? I'll give you £50 for every referral that signs up."

---

## Golden rules
- Always lead with **their** benefit (more customers, no commission). Never lead with "I make websites".
- Personalise **every** message with something specific about their business.
- Send the free sample. It does 80% of the selling.
- Be friendly, not pushy. Three messages max, then move on.
